## Nothing set up in advance

DevForge ships with no account and no server. On first launch you pick your language and your projects folder, then plug in only what you use: GitHub, a server, Vercel, databases, Forgy's key. Everything stays on your PC; keys, tokens and passwords are encrypted by Windows.

## Forgy on the desktop

**Settings › Forgy › Forgy on the desktop** (off by default) shows a bubble always on top, at the bottom right of the main screen, above the clock, even when DevForge is in the taskbar.

- **Open**: click the bubble, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>Space</kbd> from any application. If that shortcut is already taken (by the [global launcher](/devforge/en/getting-started/interface/#the-global-launcher) or another app), it is <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd>. The active shortcut shows under the switch.
- **Ask**: one line, <kbd>Enter</kbd>. The answer shows above, with **Copy** and **Open in DevForge** (the conversation goes on in the panel).
- **Dots**: if the bubble is folded during an answer, an orange dot tells it has arrived, a red one an error.
- **Chips**: the thinking depth (set for the conversation) and the project in context.
- **Fold**: the chevron, <kbd>Esc</kbd> or the shortcut. Folding after an answer starts a new conversation.

It is the same assistant as the panel: same key, same budget, same history. Folded, the bubble never takes the focus and nothing moves in it.

## Dictation

The microphone button of the panel and of the bubble lets you **speak** instead of typing: click to start, click to finish (60 seconds at most). The text is added to the field.

Recognition (Whisper) **runs on your PC**: your voice never leaves the computer and no account is needed.

| Model | Size | For |
|---|---|---|
| Fast | about 75 MB | everyday use |
| Accurate | about 250 MB | better on technical words |

The model is picked in **Settings › Forgy › Dictation**. It downloads once, on the first dictation or with **Download now**, then everything works offline. **Remove model** frees the space. The language is the interface's.

If the microphone is refused: **Windows Settings › Privacy › Microphone**.
